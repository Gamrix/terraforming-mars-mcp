Note: I started at TR 24 vs John's 20, so there was a +4 TR handicap in my favour. Without it the margin is about +11.

## Corporations & Key Cards

- **Claude**: PhoboLog + Metals Company + Experimental Forest. Io Mining Industries played Gen 1 for 10 Ti + 1 MC. Sponsors,
  Immigration Shuttles, Space Hotels, Miranda Resort, Toll Station (MC production 19–24). Soletta + Mohole Area (heat

- **John**: Tharsis Republic. Energy engine (Power Generation, Power Grid → 11 energy), Ore Processor, Martian Rails,

  Small Animals, Interstellar Colony Ship, Big Asteroid / Giant Ice Asteroid.

## What Went Right

### 1. Awards: 10 VP (two self-funded award wins, the best award result so far)
- **Thermalist** funded Gen 7 at 8 MC. My lead was structural: 11–12 heat production (Soletta + Mohole) against John's 1.
  In the last 2 gens I stopped converting heat to protect it. Final projection was 35 vs 26.
- **Landlord** funded Gen 10 as the 3rd award (20 MC) with an 11–3 tile lead and at most 2 gens left.
  I followed rule #25: fund late, with a big lead.
- Key detail from the server source: before final production, Thermalist scores **heat + energy + heat production**.
  After final production it scores heat only. Leftover energy turns into heat at production.

### 2. Two milestones (10 VP) under a 3-cap race
- Gardener in Gen 6: Imported Nitrogen's plants gave the 3rd greenery, claimed right away.
- Mayor in Gen 9: John had Builder claimable for 3+ gens but never claimed it. As first player I played Immigrant City
  (3rd city) and claimed Mayor in the same turn, which shut John out of a 2nd milestone.

### 3. Ended the game on my terms
In Gen 11 I spent 28 MC on 2 Asteroid standard projects to max the temperature. That denied John's growing engine
(29 MC production, Power Grid, Olympus Conference, Mars University) a Gen 12.
Lesson #30 from Game 7 applied successfully.

### 4. Hate-drafting worked
I denied Equatorial Magnetizer, Magnetic Field Generators, Steelworks, Ironworks and Heat Trappers. John had 6–11 energy
production all game with nowhere efficient to spend it except Ore Processor and Martian Rails.

### 5. Triangle city cluster in the south
Cities at 42, 55 and 57 shared greenery spots 49, 50, 56 and 48. Greenery 50 touched all 3 cities, and the final
greenery 48 touched 2.

## What Went Wrong

1. **Lost my first city spot.** I placed greeneries at 30 and 38 first. John then put cities at 21 and 39 next to them,
   which gave him about 4 adjacency VP from my tiles. Place a city before greeneries, or keep greeneries on board edges.
2. **Birds timing.** I played Birds when John had 0 plant production, so the -2 hit my own. John played Worms (+3 plant
   production) right after. Hold Birds until the opponent has plant production, unless the game is ending.
3. **Plant attacks again.** Big Asteroid (-4) and Giant Ice Asteroid (-6) destroyed 10 plants. No Protected Habitats seen.
4. **Card VP was still slightly behind (28 vs 29)**, even though I won overall.

## Key Lessons

1. **Build heat production for Thermalist.** Soletta + Mohole gave an uncatchable lead. Stop converting heat in the final 1–2 gens.
2. **Fund the 3rd award late when the lead is large.** Landlord at 20 MC with an 11–3 lead and ≤2 gens left is about 4 MC/VP.
3. **PhoboLog + Io Mining Industries on Gen 1** (10 Ti = 40 MC) was a great opening. Add Jovian cards (Water Import From
   Europa, Miranda Resort, Callisto Penal Mines) for VP-per-Jovian stacking.
4. **Watch the opponent's unclaimed milestones.** If they sit on a claimable milestone, grab the last slot with a
   one-turn claim sequence.
5. **Ending the game when ahead beats building more.** Two Asteroid standard projects (28 MC) denied John a full generation.
